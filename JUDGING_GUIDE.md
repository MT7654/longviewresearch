## One-paragraph description

Longview Research is a guided equity-education agent. A learner chooses a global company and records their opening hypothesis. Longview automatically resolves the listing, scans linked public coverage, measures narrative breadth, separates evidence from interpretation, and runs only the deterministic valuation, factor, and risk methods supported by the data. It synthesises an Independent Educational Opinion Piece whose model boundary is as visible as its conclusions. The final debrief tests whether the learner can explain the result rather than merely repeat it. Gemini is optional and bounded to plain-language critique; tested code owns every canonical number and a deterministic fallback preserves the complete journey when free-tier inference is unavailable.

## Why it fits the Life track

Longview develops an individual's financial capability. The intended outcome is a learner who can:

- separate a company story from evidence;
- explain why valuation methods disagree;
- understand what a price mathematically assumes;
- spot missing or unsuitable model inputs;
- identify evidence that would weaken a thesis; and
- communicate uncertainty without false precision.

## The product thesis

Most investment content begins with an answer. Longview begins with the learner's belief and makes the analytical journey visible.

The hypothesis is pedagogical context only. It changes which misconceptions the app explains; it never changes model inputs, valuation output, or the publication's model opinion.

Show reverse DCF, scenario DCF, relative value, seeded Monte Carlo range, factor proxies, and historical risk.

- dated model range;

### 3:20–4:00 — Debrief and export

Complete the three questions.

Show how the debrief reconnects the evidence and models to the learner's starting hypothesis. Unlock and print the complete Independent Educational Opinion Piece.

## What makes the AI use substantive

The agent performs a bounded multi-stage workflow:

1. exact-listing resolution;
2. coverage assessment;
3. evidence classification;
4. deterministic model execution;
5. competing-case synthesis;
6. hypothesis-aware teaching;
7. optional Gemini critique;
8. deterministic fallback;
9. comprehension gating; and
10. versioned printable publication.

The LLM cannot:

- create a canonical financial input;
- calculate the published range;
- override a coverage boundary;
- use the learner's hypothesis to bias an opinion; or
- produce personalised action language.

## Judging alignment

### AI Innovation — 30%

- The learner's hypothesis becomes a continuous teaching thread without contaminating the model.
- AI critique is constrained by deterministic truth and explicit editorial layers.
- The full workflow degrades gracefully to a local tutor when Gemini is rate-limited.
- The output is a verifiable learning artifact, not a transient chat response.

### Technical Excellence — 20%

- Exact global listing and currency resolution.
- Explicit complete, partial, and unsupported coverage states.
- Numerical reverse solving and seeded Monte Carlo reproducibility.
- Formula, sample, policy-layer, and advice-language tests.
- Server-side key handling, bounded retry, cache, cooldown, and model fallback.

### User Experience — 25%

- Only a stock and optional hypothesis are needed.
- The roadmap is automatic and educational.
- Progressive disclosure keeps advanced quant controls optional.
- A hedge-fund-inspired visual language remains readable for non-professionals.
- The debrief provides a clear finish and printable artifact.

### Business Value — 25%

- Addresses a large gap between superficial finance content and expensive professional research tools.
- Works without brokerage integration or personal financial data.
- Supports global discovery with an honest partial-data state.
- Creates a credible path to consumer education, institutional sponsorship of literacy, or subscription publishing after licensing and legal review.

## Defined impact

These are implementation targets, not claimed user-study results:

- every model output is traceable to visible inputs;
- every opinion section identifies its editorial layer;
- no personal suitability data is collected;
- no buy, sell, or hold label is produced;
- the full sample journey works without credentials;
- the calculation journey works without Gemini;
- learning is measured with a three-question check.

### Product sharing

> I used Tencent Cloud CodeBuddy to convert an earlier market-entry research product into the foundation of a consumer investment-learning agent. CodeBuddy accelerated repository mapping and the initial cross-codebase pivot. The process also showed why agent-generated software needs explicit mathematical tests, data-provenance rules, and product-scope review. Longview's final architecture therefore separates Gemini's bounded explanatory role from deterministic valuation code, allowing every canonical output to remain reproducible and the demo to keep working when free-tier inference is rate-limited.
